# Episode 01 — Audio Production Map

Runtime: 01:36

## Mix direction

- Warm, curious cartoon-fantasy tone
- Mystery should feel intriguing rather than frightening
- Dialogue/narration space is intentionally preserved in the center channel
- Music stays low so Thai voices can be added later without rebuilding the picture edit

## Voice plan

Thai narration and character dialogue remain unrendered in this version because
the workstation currently has only English system voices installed. Recommended
voice roles:

- Narrator: warm adult voice, intimate storybook delivery
- Noah: curious young voice, natural and understated
- Aster: calm adult voice with quiet authority
- Unseen thief: soft processed whisper; identity and gender must remain unclear
