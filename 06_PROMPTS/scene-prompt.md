# Scene Prompt Bible

# Overall Art Direction

Style:

Fantasy anime cinematic

Mood:

* mysterious
* emotional
* magical
* warm

Visual Reference:

Anime fantasy world mixed with magical realism.

---

# Human World

## Description

A normal modern small town.

Quiet streets.

Warm sunset lighting.

A slightly nostalgic feeling.

## Emotion

Ordinary life before discovering something extraordinary.

---

# Lost Village

## Description

A hidden fantasy village where forgotten things live.

Old wooden houses.

Warm glowing lights.

Ancient pathways.

Magical objects everywhere.

## Atmosphere

A place that feels:

* forgotten
* peaceful
* mysterious
* alive

---

# Lost Door

## Description

An ancient wooden door standing alone.

Covered with mysterious symbols.

Surrounded by darkness.

A warm magical light coming from behind the door.

## Emotion

The feeling of:

"Something unknown is waiting."

---

# Visual Consistency Rules

Always maintain:

* fantasy anime style
* cinematic lighting
* emotional atmosphere
* detailed environment
* magical but believable world

Avoid:

* modern sci-fi style
* futuristic technology
* random character redesign
* different art styles
