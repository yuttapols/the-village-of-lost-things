# Noah

## Character Identity

Name:

Noah

Role:

Main Protagonist

Age:

16 years old

Personality:

* Kind
* Quiet
* Observant
* Curious
* Emotional but hides feelings

---

## Visual Description

A 16-year-old boy with black hair and deep brown eyes.

He has a gentle face with a slightly lonely expression.

His appearance is simple and ordinary.

He wears a dark hoodie and casual clothes.

He should look like a normal teenager,
not a hero at first glance.

---

## Character Feeling

The image should communicate:

* loneliness
* curiosity
* hidden potential
* kindness

---

## Never Change

Must always keep:

* black hair
* brown eyes
* teenage appearance
* simple clothing style
* calm expression

# Aster

## Character Identity

Name:

Aster

Role:

Keeper of Lost Village

---

## Visual Description

A mysterious young man with silver hair and deep blue eyes.

Tall figure.

Wears a dark ancient cloak with mysterious symbols.

His expression is calm and serious.

He looks like someone who has carried secrets for a very long time.

---

## Character Feeling

The image should communicate:

* wisdom
* mystery
* responsibility
* loneliness

---

## Never Change

Must always keep:

* silver hair
* blue eyes
* dark cloak
* serious expression

---

# Kabi

## Character Identity

Name:

Cabinara (Kabi)

Role:

Guardian and first friend of Noah

---

## Visual Description

A cute fantasy creature.

Small round body.

Soft brown cream fur.

Large round eyes.

Small ears.

Short legs.

A fluffy round tail.

Looks lazy and adorable.

---

## Character Feeling

The image should communicate:

* cute
* friendly
* harmless
* secretly powerful

---

## Hidden Contrast

Normal:

Cute and sleepy

Battle:

Powerful guardian protecting friends

---

## Never Change

Must always keep:

* round body
* brown cream color
* cute appearance
* sleepy expression
