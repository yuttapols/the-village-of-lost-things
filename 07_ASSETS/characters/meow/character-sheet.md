# Character Sheet

# Meow

## Role

Ramber's cat companion and party tracker

First Appearance:

Season 1 Episode 3

---

## Visual Identity

* Small compact fluffy cat
* Round slightly flat face
* Cream-white fur
* Pale ginger-orange patches
* Golden-green half-lidded eyes
* Pink nose
* Thick fluffy tail
* Deep teal scarf
* Antique-silver star-circle tag

## Personality

Looks unimpressed,
but is clever,
loyal,
and secretly affectionate.

## Consistency Rules

Always preserve:

* facial shape and serious expression
* cream and ginger fur pattern
* teal scarf
* silver tag
* one fluffy tail

Avoid:

* aggressive monster features
* changing eye color
* collar bell
* clothing beyond the scarf
