# Meow Image Prompt

## Master Prompt

A small fluffy companion cat named Meow,
round slightly flat face,
cream-white long fur,
pale ginger-orange patches,
golden-green half-lidded eyes,
small pink nose,
thick fluffy ginger-ringed tail,
wearing a deep teal scarf with a tiny antique-silver star-circle tag.

Meow looks dignified,
mildly unimpressed,
adorable,
clever,
and secretly affectionate.

Family-friendly 2D cartoon fantasy animation,
rounded clean linework,
soft simplified fur,
gentle cel shading,
consistent original companion design.

## Avoid

No extra animals,
no bell,
no wings,
no clothing beyond the scarf,
no text,
no watermark.
