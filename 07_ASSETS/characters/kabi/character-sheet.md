# Character Concept

Kabi is a mysterious creature living in Lost Village.

At first glance, he looks like a lazy and harmless little creature.

He loves sleeping, eating, and avoiding problems.

However, behind his cute appearance is one of the strongest guardians of Lost Village.

When someone important to him is threatened,
Kabi reveals his true power.
