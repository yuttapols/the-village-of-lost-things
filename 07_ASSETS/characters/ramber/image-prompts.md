# Ramber Image Prompt

## Master Prompt

A confident 10-year-old Lost Village apprentice named Ramber,
short black hair,
warm medium-brown skin,
dark brown eyes,
compact child build,
wearing a deep teal hooded vest over a rust-orange shirt,
dark navy adventure shorts and leggings,
brown boots,
fingerless gloves,
and a recovery satchel with a small finder's rod.

He looks brave, playful, clever, and slightly competitive.

Family-friendly 2D cartoon fantasy animation,
rounded clean linework,
gentle cel shading,
warm emotional storytelling,
consistent original character design.

## Avoid

No logos,
no superhero graphics,
no readable text,
no adult proportions,
no armor,
no sword,
no watermark.
